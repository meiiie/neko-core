`Neko Core` name, the official Neko Core logos, and their distinctive official
branding (the "Marks"). These marks are not declared registered marks unless
an official registration says so.

The AGPL and Apache licenses cover copyright and patent permissions in their
respective code. They do not grant permission to use the Marks.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- truthfully refer to Neko Core;
- link to the official project;
- state that another product is compatible with Neko Core, if accurate; and
- retain factual attribution when redistributing an unmodified official copy.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official
status.

## Uses that require written permission

Without written permission, do not use the Marks:

- as the name, logo, domain, package identifier, or app-store identity of a
  modified distribution or competing product;
- in merchandise, paid advertising, or commercial services in a way that
  suggests an official offering; or
- in a confusingly similar visual identity.

Forks and modified distributions must use a distinct name and branding and
must identify themselves as unofficial. A modified distribution that removes
or changes Neko Core's founding principle in `docs/process/SOVEREIGNTY.md`
must not use the Neko Core Marks.

Nothing in this policy limits rights that applicable law grants without the
owner's permission, including legitimate nominative or descriptive fair use.
